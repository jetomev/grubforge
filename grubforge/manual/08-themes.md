# Themes

## Use a theme

On **Themes** (press **4**): pick one, look at the preview, **Use This Theme**, then **F10** and **Save and Rebuild**. **Stop Using a Theme** goes back to the plain menu.

The preview is approximate: it shows your real entries in the theme's colours. Fonts and pictures appear only at the real start-up.

## Install a theme you downloaded

1. Download a theme: a folder with a `theme.txt` inside, or the `.tar.gz` / `.zip` it came in.
2. **Themes ▸ Install a Theme (i)** (or press **I**). **Choose** lists what's in Downloads; or type a path.
3. **Install**. It's copied into GRUB's themes folder; you're asked for your password.

Only the theme's plain files are copied: links, and anything trying to reach outside the theme's folder, are refused. Nothing from the theme is run.
