# Start-up settings

How the menu behaves when the computer starts. In **Settings ▸ Start-up**.

## Choose what starts first

1. Open **Settings** (press **2**), then **Start-up**.
2. On **Start this entry**, press **Enter** and pick an entry. Type to jump to it.
3. Press **F10**, check the review, choose **Save and rebuild**.

You can also pick an entry on the **Boot menu** screen and press **Start this first**.

## The settings

- **Start this entry**: what starts when nobody presses a key. **Last chosen** starts whatever was picked the time before; it needs **Remember last choice** on too.
- **Remember last choice**: makes **Last chosen** work.
- **Wait before starting**: seconds the menu waits. **0** starts at once; **wait forever** waits until you pick. Use the preset buttons or type a number; **↑ ↓** step through the presets.
- **Show the menu**: **Always**; **With a countdown** (only the seconds; press **Esc** to see the menu); **Hidden** (hold **Shift**, or press **Esc**, while starting to see it).

GRUB names on this page: `GRUB_DEFAULT`, `GRUB_SAVEDEFAULT`, `GRUB_TIMEOUT`, `GRUB_TIMEOUT_STYLE`.
